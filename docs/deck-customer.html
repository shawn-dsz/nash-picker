Demo · substitutions

The customer already decided

  • Chosen at checkout, not in the aisle. The picker confirms in one tap
  • They see a product, not a code. Name and picture of the approved swap
  • “Refund, don't substitute” shows no alternative at all

Handing someone a replacement they explicitly declined is worse than handing them nothing.

Demo · scan to confirm

The wrong item cannot be picked

  • Scan the Classic against a Zero line. It is rejected, by name
  • Out of stock needs no scan. You cannot scan a thing that is not there
  • Damaged label? Override. Allowed, and recorded rather than silent

Real handheld scanners type like a keyboard, so this works on the hardware your stores already have.

Ownership

What I deliberately did not build

Batch pickingSeveral orders in one trip. The single biggest throughput lever, and the obvious next release. Out today because one order end to end had to be right first
OfflineA supermarket drops signal in the freezer aisle. Stated as a requirement, not built. Half a day of work
Two pickers, one orderNothing currently stops it. The fix is a claim step, and it is the first thing I would add for a pilot
Indoor navigationNeeds beacons in the building. Out on cost, not on difficulty
